Method & Decision Rule

Core Mathematical Rule

  • Spread Calculation: Spreadt = PriceA,t − PriceB,t
  • Historical Baseline: Mean (μ) and Population Std Dev (σ) computed over the historical observation window (all days before the latest, or the rolling 10-day window when selected).
  • Anomaly Trigger: | (Spreadt − μ) / σ | ≥ threshold (default: 2.0σ)
  • Liquidity Filter: Contract A Volume ≥ min volume filter (default: 1,000 units).
